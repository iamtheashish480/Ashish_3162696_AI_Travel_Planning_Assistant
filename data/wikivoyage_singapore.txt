Title: Wikivoyage Singapore Travel Guide
URL: https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Singapore

Attractions & Districts:
- Marina Bay Sands: Features the Sands SkyPark, Observation Deck, and Gardens by the Bay nearby.
- Chinatown: Cultural enclave featuring Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Sri Mariamman Temple, and Maxwell Food Centre.
- Kampong Gelam: Historic Malay district home to Sultan Mosque, Arab Street, and colorful textiles.
- Little India: Vibrant neighbourhood with Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple and Mustafa Centre.
- Sentosa Island: Resort island with beaches, Universal Studios Singapore, and S.E.A. Aquarium.

Transportation:
- Mass Rapid Transit (MRT): Most efficient way to travel across Singapore. Ez-Link card or contactless Visa/Mastercard accepted.
- Buses: Comprehensive bus network complementing the MRT.
- Taxis & Ridesharing: Grab, Gojek, and Tada are widely used ride-hailing apps.

Practical Tips:
- Tap water is safe to drink.
- Chewing gum sale is prohibited.
- Tipping is not customary as a 10% service charge is included in most restaurant bills.
