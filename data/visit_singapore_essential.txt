Title: Visit Singapore: Essential Visitor Information
URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com/travel-guide-tips/essential-information/

Climate:
- Singapore has a tropical climate with high humidity and warm temperatures year-round (24°C to 32°C).
- Sudden rainfall and afternoon thunderstorms are common. Always carry an umbrella.

Indoor Attractions for Rainy Days:
- S.E.A. Aquarium (Sentosa)
- ArtScience Museum (Marina Bay)
- Jewel Changi Airport (Rain Vortex & Canopy Park)
- National Gallery Singapore
- Asian Civilisations Museum
