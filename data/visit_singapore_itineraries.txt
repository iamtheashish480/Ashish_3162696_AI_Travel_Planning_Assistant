Title: Visit Singapore: Sample Itineraries
URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com/itineraries/

3-Day Highlights Itinerary:
- Day 1: Explore Civic District, National Gallery, Merlion Park, and Gardens by the Bay (Supertree Grove).
- Day 2: Cultural immersion in Chinatown (Buddha Tooth Relic Temple), Kampong Gelam, and Little India.
- Day 3: Sentosa Island relaxation, Universal Studios, or S.E.A. Aquarium, ending with Jewel Changi Airport.

Family & Kids Recommendations:
- Singapore Zoo, Night Safari, and River Wonders.
- Jacobs Ballas Children's Garden.
- Universal Studios Singapore.
